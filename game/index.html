Glowmoth

A little moth. A dark garden. Every lamp waiting for light.

Goal — WIN: light every lamp to clear the level. LOSE: your 3 lives run out.

Steer with the mouse or WASD / arrows. Space dashes (dash through a bat to scare it off!).

Pollen builds your combo — grab it quickly for up to ×8 points — and banks into a shop between levels.

Moon: every level the moon changes phase and the garden changes with it — full moon doubles pollen, new moon slows the bats.

Boss: every 5th level the MOTH-EATER hunts you — 3 dash-hits to scare it off for +500.

Three lives. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts. 1–4 buy in the shop.
