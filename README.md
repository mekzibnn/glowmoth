Bloomwarden — the last spore-wisp blooms glowcap mushrooms against the Blight across 7 blooms. Steer with the mouse or WASD/arrows, dash with Space (dashing through a mite scares it off), collect spores to build a chain up to ×8 and bank them for the draft. Bloom every glowcap to clear the bloom — that's the WIN; clear bloom 7 and the dawn breaks. Lose all your hearts and the Blight wins. Each bloom adds glowcaps and faster mites in 4 kinds (Mite, Dart, Spitter, Bulwark), and every bloom the sporefall phase turns and the grove changes with it: FULLBLOOM doubles spores, DORMANT slows the mites. Bosses hunt you on blooms 3, 5 and 7 — THORNMAW, BLIGHTMAW, and THE ROTKING (5 dash-hits) — dash through them to scare them off. After each bloom, draft: pick 1 of 3 relics (12 in all, common / rare / epic — reroll for 15 spores) and build your run. Three difficulty tides (GENTLE ×0.75, WILDWOOD ×1, BLIGHTSTORM ×1.5), 8 feats, a grade (S/A/B/C) and a score-by-bloom chart on the victory screen, best scores per tide, and synthesized sound throughout — no assets, no network, just canvas and WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
